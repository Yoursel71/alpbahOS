in its hover colour, so it said "app open: no" for the reopened window in rounds 47x, 48 and 48g although the window
was open. It now checks two points inside the button off the label; re-checked on the 18 saved W4 screenshots of
rounds 48 and 48g, every verdict matches the picture.
