## Seen and not fixed

- Firefox settings say "Firefox varsayılan tarayıcınız değil" although `/etc/xdg/mimeapps.list` makes `firefox.desktop`
  the handler for http/https/html (the vendor pref only hides the start-up prompt).
- LibreOffice's first-start dialog has English tab titles ("Welcome", "User Interface", "Appearance"): untranslated
  strings in LibreOffice 26.2's Turkish, not an alpbahOS file.
- VLC asks its privacy/network question at the first start (Turkish); kept as VLC's own consent step.
- The bundled Firefox codecs, LibreOffice's Impress media (no GStreamer), LDAP address books and database drivers
  were not tested or are not built (above).
- Size: the ISO grew by about 360 MB (apps about +1.1 GB, `/root` leftovers −550 MB).

Not run: real hardware; Firefox video; printing; spelling check in Writer.
